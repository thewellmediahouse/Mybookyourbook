# Cursor instruction

Analyze the current website and improve the homepage based on this direction:

We are **The Well Media House** — a premium media and business growth company. We specialize in professional website design, cinematic company promo videos, product photography, product demo videos, marketing strategies, and business growth systems.

Do not make the site look like a generic digital marketing agency. It must feel premium, cinematic, strategic, and trustworthy. Use a luxury dark navy and gold visual direction.

## Main goal

Make the website clearly communicate that we help businesses look professional, build trust, get more visibility, get more bookings, and grow revenue.

## Design improvements

1. Keep the dark navy/gold premium theme, but make the layout feel more high-end and less box-heavy.
2. Increase spacing between sections.
3. Use larger, more confident typography in the hero.
4. Add more cinematic visual depth using soft gold glows, subtle gradients, blurred light streaks, and premium image cards.
5. Make the hero feel more like a premium media company, not only a growth chart background.
6. Use image-led service and portfolio cards so the website proves visual quality immediately.
7. Add clear calls to action throughout: **Book a Consultation**, **View Our Work**, and **Watch Showreel**.

## Animation rules

Add subtle, premium animations only:

- Fade-up sections on scroll.
- Card hover lift: `transform: translateY(-6px)`.
- Image hover scale: `transform: scale(1.04)`.
- Soft gold glow on primary CTA hover.
- No childish bouncing, spinning, or loud animations.

## Responsive rules

Desktop:

- Hero should be 2 columns.
- Services should be 3 columns.
- Portfolio should be 3 columns.
- Growth path cards should be 3 columns.

Tablet:

- Hero can stay 2 columns if readable, otherwise stack.
- Cards become 2 columns.

Mobile:

- Hero stacks text first, showreel second.
- Buttons become full width.
- Cards become 1 column.
- Navigation becomes hamburger.
- Keep headline readable and avoid text over busy image areas.

## Important final instruction

Do not remove the premium feel. Do not clutter the homepage with too much text. Make the site sell the idea that The Well Media House helps businesses look professional, trusted, and ready for growth.
